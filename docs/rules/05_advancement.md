# XP & Levels

As you level up you get rewards according to the table below.

## Gaining XP
- You get **1 XP** for each silver piece worth of treasure or coin that you loot from the dungeon.
- You also get XP for defeating enemies and completing quests (10 XP for something easy up to 200+ for a really tough one).
- **Mainly it’s about the looting.**
